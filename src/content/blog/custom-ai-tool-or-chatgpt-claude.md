We build custom AI software for a living, and we still tell people to start with ChatGPT or Claude. It costs almost nothing to find out whether AI helps with your job, and the result tells you what is worth building.

## When ChatGPT or Claude is enough

- **It is occasional.** Drafting a tricky email, summarising a long document, brainstorming. You do it now and then.
- **One person does it.** There is no shared process to keep consistent.
- **You are happy to paste things in.** The information fits in a chat and is not sensitive in a way that rules that out.
- **The answer does not have to be the same every time.** A different phrasing each time is fine.

If that describes your use, a chat tool, perhaps with a saved set of instructions, is probably all you need. Do not build software to solve a problem that a good prompt already solves.

## Signs you have outgrown the chat window

- **You keep doing the same thing.** Same steps, same copy and paste, every week. That is the strongest signal.
- **It needs your data.** The useful answer depends on your customer records, documents or systems, and pasting them in by hand is slow or risky.
- **Several people need to do it the same way.** Without a shared tool you get five people with five slightly different prompts and five slightly different results.
- **It has to be consistent and checkable.** You need the same format every time, a record of what was produced, or a person to approve before anything goes out.
- **It should happen without anyone starting it.** An email arrives and something happens, with nobody opening a chat.

## A simple test

For a month, keep a note of every task you use AI for and how often. At the end:

- Tasks you did once or twice: leave them in the chat window.
- Tasks you did weekly or daily, in the same way: candidates for a custom tool or an automation.
- Tasks that involve your own data or several people: the best candidates of all.

## Custom tool or automation?

They are different things. An [automation](/services/ai-automation/) moves information and triggers actions between the tools you already have, with AI steps where judgement is needed. A [custom AI tool](/services/custom-ai-software-development/) is software built around your process when nothing off the shelf fits. Many jobs need a bit of both, and often the right answer is the smaller one.

## What it takes to do it properly

A tool you rely on needs more than a clever prompt: testing against your real cases, error handling, documentation, a handover and someone to look after it. That is the difference between a demo and software, and it is why we do it the way we do.
